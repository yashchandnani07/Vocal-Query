How it works

Four steps from voice to answer.

No query editor, no syntax. You talk, the app does the SQL.

01

Speak

Tap the orb and ask in plain English. Your mic audio streams straight to Gemini Live.

"Which city has the most customers?"
02

Gemini writes SQL

Gemini sees your table and column names and calls a run_sql tool with a query.

SELECT city, COUNT(*) ...
03

You confirm changes

SELECTs run right away. INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE wait for your yes first.

Delete 1 row? Yes / No
04

See + hear the answer

Rows appear in a table and the answer is spoken back. The query is saved to voice_log.

"Mumbai, with 3 customers."
